Fictional company & concept

Seabright Realty Studio

Every inquiry. A clear next step.

  • CRM integrations
  • AI-assisted drafts

The everyday friction

Buyer inquiries arrive through listing sites, email, and texts. Follow-ups live in different places, so the next step is hard to see.

What we could build

A shared inquiry workspace that connects lead sources, prepares reply drafts, and puts every next action on the agent’s calendar.

Explore the proposed workflow+
  1. Bring incoming inquiries into one queue, with their source and property context.
  2. Suggest a reply using approved listing information; the agent reviews before sending.
  3. Create a follow-up task and keep notes connected to the contact.

Illustrative scope. Any real implementation starts with discovery, testing, and your team’s review.

Fictional company & concept

Juniper Table

Less chasing. More hospitality.

  • Inquiry automation
  • Team handoffs

The everyday friction

Catering requests get buried in the inbox while menus, availability, and the service team’s notes sit in separate tools.

What we could build

A catering inquiry and operations hub that collects the right details, prepares a quote, and gives the team one shared event brief.

Explore the proposed workflow+
  1. Collect event date, guest count, dietary requests, and contact details in one form.
  2. Build a draft quote from the restaurant’s current menu and pricing.
  3. Let the manager approve it, then hand the confirmed brief to the kitchen.

Illustrative scope. Any real implementation starts with discovery, testing, and your team’s review.

The everyday friction

Harborlight Care Studio

A smoother day at the front desk.

  • Administrative workflows
  • Human review

The everyday friction

The administrative team checks forms, routes scheduling requests, and follows up on missing information across multiple queues.

What we could build

A staff-facing administrative workspace for intake checklists and scheduling handoffs, designed around permissions and human review.

Explore the proposed workflow+
  1. Show staff a checklist of required administrative forms and outstanding items.
  2. Route scheduling requests to the appropriate team for review.
  3. Record task ownership and activity, with access limited to the relevant staff.

Illustrative scope. Any real implementation starts with discovery, testing, and your team’s review.
