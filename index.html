What does “forward-deployed” mean?

We work alongside your team, learning the real workflow from the people doing the work. That understanding guides what we build, how we test it, and how we put it into production.

Do we need to know exactly what to build?

No. Start with a problem, a slow workflow, or a goal. We work with your team to understand it, decide what is worth solving first, and define a practical first step.

Can you work with our existing systems?

Yes. Integrations are a core part of our work. We look at the APIs, data, and access your systems provide, then design a solution that connects what you already use.

How do you approach AI projects?

We start with a specific business problem. Whether it calls for an assistant, an agent, a document pipeline, or a predictive model, we scope the work, set up evaluations, and test it before it goes live.

Where do you work?

We are based in San Diego and work with teams across Southern California and remotely. We shape the collaboration around the people and the work.
