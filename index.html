Forward-deployed

We work where the work happens

A lot of business software is built from a requirements document by people who never see the job it is meant to support. The result looks right in a demo and falls apart on a Tuesday afternoon.

Forward-deployed means we work inside your team for the engagement. We sit in on the real workflow, learn the edge cases, and build against what we see. The goal is working software your team uses, not a deck of recommendations.

What you get

  • Hands-on work inside your team
  • Software deployed on your systems
  • Evaluations for anything that uses AI
  • Code and documentation your team can maintain

What you do not get

  • A slide deck and a list of recommendations
  • A prototype that never ships
  • Promised savings or made-up metrics

How we work

Embed, build, ship

Every engagement follows the same three steps, whether it is an internal tool, an AI assistant, or a new app.

  1. 1

    Embed

    We join your team and learn the workflow first-hand: the tools, the exceptions, and the steps that only live in someone's head.

  2. 2

    Build

    We build in short cycles inside your environment and put working versions in front of the people who will use them. For AI work, we set up evaluations early so quality is measured, not guessed.

  3. 3

    Ship

    We deploy to production, watch how it holds up under real use, fix what needs fixing, and hand over code and documentation your team can run.

A good first engagement is a single workflow. Tell us which one costs your team the most time.

FAQ

Common questions

Short answers about forward-deployed work, enterprise applications, AI and machine learning, and websites and apps.

What does Coastline Digital do?

We are a forward-deployed AI firm that specializes in enterprise application solutions. We work alongside your team to build internal tools, workflow automation, integrations, and AI and machine learning systems. We also build websites and apps.

What does forward-deployed mean?

We work inside your team instead of handing over a plan from the outside. We learn the real workflow, build against it, and stay through deployment and rollout.

What kinds of AI work do you take on?

Applied AI with a clear job to do: LLM-powered assistants and agents, document and data pipelines, and predictive or machine learning models. We set up evaluations so you can see how well it works, then deploy it.

Do we have to replace our existing systems?

No. We would rather connect to the systems you already run and fill the gaps between them. If something does need replacing, we will say so and explain why.

How much does it cost?

We do not publish fixed prices here. Tell us what you need and we will talk through options.
