Websites and apps

The public side of the business deserves the same care as the internal tools. We build web and mobile apps, marketing sites that say clearly what you do, and Google Business Profiles that match the real business.

What it covers

  • Web and mobile app builds. Customer-facing apps and portals, built and shipped the same way as our internal tools.
  • Marketing sites. Fast, mobile-first sites with a clear message and a real contact path.
  • Google Business Profile cleanup. Categories, services, photos, hours, and contact details that match the real business.

How it runs

  1. 1 Embed. You tell us who the site or app is for and what they need to do.
  2. 2 Build. We propose a simple structure and build working versions you can click through.
  3. 3 Ship. We launch and show your team how to keep it up to date.

What we do not do

  • Invent reviews, rankings, or traffic claims
  • Put personal home addresses or personal phone numbers on public assets
