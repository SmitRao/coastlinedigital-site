# Coastline Digital
> Coastline Digital is a forward-deployed AI and software firm focused on enterprise applications. We work alongside your team, learn how the work actually gets done, and build software meant to hold up in daily production use.

## What we build
- Enterprise applications: custom internal tools, workflow automation, and integrations that connect the systems you already use. https://coastlinedigital.net/services/enterprise-applications/
- AI and machine learning: assistants and agents, document and data pipelines, and predictive models, each scoped to a real business problem and tested before it goes live. https://coastlinedigital.net/services/ai-machine-learning/
- Websites and apps: web and mobile applications and marketing sites. https://coastlinedigital.net/services/websites-apps/

## How we work
- Understand: spend time with the people doing the work and learn how their workflows actually run.
- Focus: choose the few problems worth solving first and define a useful first release.
- Build and ship: work in short cycles so teams see working results early, with testing and a considered production rollout.
