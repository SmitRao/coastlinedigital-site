Home FAQ

Questions, answered.

What we build, how we work alongside your team, and what to expect from an engagement.

What does Coastline Digital do?

We are a forward-deployed AI and software firm focused on enterprise applications. We build custom internal tools, workflow automation, integrations, AI and machine learning systems, and websites and apps.

What does forward-deployed mean?

We work alongside your team and spend time with the people doing the work. We learn how your workflows actually run, build software around them, and stay involved through deployment and rollout.

How does an engagement start?

Every engagement starts with understanding your people, systems, and day-to-day work. Together, we pick the few problems worth solving first and define a focused first build. We then ship in short cycles so you see working results early.

What kinds of enterprise applications do you build?

We build internal tools such as dashboards, approval queues, and admin panels, automate repetitive workflows, and connect the systems you already use. Learn more about our enterprise applications.

What kinds of AI work do you take on?

Assistants and agents, document and data pipelines, and predictive models. Each project is scoped to a real business problem and tested against examples from your workflow before it goes live. Explore our AI and machine learning work.

How do you evaluate AI before launch?

We define what a useful result looks like with your team, build an evaluation set from real examples, and measure each version against it. We review the results together and plan monitoring and human review around the task.

Can you work with our existing systems?

Yes. We start with the tools and systems you already use, review the available APIs and data access, and design integrations around your environment. Hosting, access, and data requirements are agreed with your team as part of the scope.

Do you also build websites and mobile apps?

Yes. We design and build web and mobile applications and marketing sites. The same collaborative process applies: understand your users, build in short cycles, and test before launch. See websites and apps.

What do we have at the end of an engagement?

Working software deployed to your agreed environment, along with the code, documentation, and handoff your team needs to run and maintain it. Rollout and ongoing support are planned as part of the engagement.

How is a project priced?

We scope the work around your goals, systems, and the first useful release. Share what you have in mind and we will discuss the scope, delivery approach, and pricing with you.

Where do you work?

We are based in San Diego and work with teams across Southern California and remotely. We agree on a working rhythm that fits your team and the project.

How do I get started?

Tell us about what your team is trying to accomplish. Use the contact form or email hello@coastlinedigital.net to start a conversation.
