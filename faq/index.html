Coastline Digital FAQ

Short answers for Southern California shops and agents comparing local websites, Google Business cleanup, and finished listing or apartment tour video.

What does Coastline Digital do?

We build and clean up websites for local SoCal businesses, polish Google Business profiles, and deliver finished listing or apartment tour videos from photos or footage you already have.

Who is this for?

Local shops and service businesses that need a clear site and Google presence, and agents or property teams that want a finished tour video without hiring a film crew.

Do you only work in Southern California?

SoCal is the focus. If you are nearby and unsure, email hello@coastlinedigital.net and we will say honestly whether we can help.

What is Google Business polish?

Cleanup and clarity on your Google Business Profile so the basics look trustworthy: categories, services, photos, hours, and how people contact you. We do not invent reviews or fake rankings.

How do listing or apartment tour videos work?

You send photos or footage you already have. We turn that into a finished tour-style video you can use on listings and social. Turnaround depends on what you send.

Do you run paid ads?

We focus on the site and Google Business first. Paid ads are separate and only if you ask.

How do I contact you?

Use the form on coastlinedigital.net or email hello@coastlinedigital.net. We do not publish a personal phone number on the site.

How much does it cost?

We do not publish fixed prices here. Tell us what you need and we will talk through options.

Should I fix my website, Google Business, or listing video first?

If people cannot find or trust you nearby, start with Google Business polish and a clear contact path on a simple site. If you already get listing traffic and need a tour video, start with video from the photos or footage you have. We will recommend an order after you tell us the goal.

Do you invent listing details in tour videos?

No. We edit from the photos or footage you provide and do not invent square footage, price, or property facts.

Is Coastline the same company as Clearline Home Filters?

No. Coastline Digital is for SoCal local sites, Google Business polish, and listing or apartment tour video. Clearline Home Filters is a separate water filtration brand.
